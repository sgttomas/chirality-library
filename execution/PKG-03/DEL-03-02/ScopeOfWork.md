## Purpose and Objective Traceability

Supply the executable part of the optional local work-and-repository evaluation
capability accepted through SCA-20261101-UNDERTAKING-EVALUATION. A project can
inspect identified repository state changes, the conditions and grounds under
which they are useful, and relevant observed effort; it can compare selected
means without invented data, comparability or authority.

Repository state change is the primary evaluative subject. Work observations
help establish relevant effort and possible means of producing that change.
Activity counts alone are not the benefit. A useful state condition can become
a local goal and an improvement hypothesis; its wider consequences remain part
of examination. The human and DEL-04-02's practice determine the meaningful
question, while this contribution makes supported observations and comparisons
inspectable and repeatable.

This contract originated as setup under the accepted amendment. PR #1799 supplies
the first executable route and bounded actual-use evidence. Its supported
interfaces, private pilot and remaining qualification limits are described in
[Work/EvaluationBasis.md](Work/EvaluationBasis.md). Implementation and passing
checks do not establish full R18/A9 fulfillment, product acceptance or release.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

A repository state basis is an identified commit, captured candidate/working
tree or issued edition with its included material and coverage stated. A state
comparison is a derived examination of selected differences and their grounds;
it is not merely a diff count or a new persistent lifecycle register. An
undertaking is the actual bounded work; its records represent selected aspects.
An attempt, retry and concurrent contribution require explicit interpretation
under the selected question rather than inference from chat boundaries alone.
A benchmark case supplies an identified starting situation and examination
basis. A trial is one actual execution under its declared configuration and
conditions. A measurement is a supported derivation from observations, distinct
from an explanation, ruling or acceptance.

evidence, not by structure, a completed process, the producer's success message
or a favourable score. Examination remains specific to source, candidate,
environment, question and intended use. Missing grounds stay visible. An actual
comparison may warrant retention, reframing or an explicit unresolved decision;
it need not demonstrate a faster variant.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Start from the accepted card, R18/A9 and the actual evaluation question. Bind the
host record/export and execution interface, selected project/task family,
permitted inputs, outcome examination, resource conditions and local retention
before dependent implementation. Select a narrow actual path; no complete event
ontology or exhaustive corpus is a prerequisite. Examine existing tools before
reusing or changing their contracts. Design choices remain revisable through
identified evidence and actual authority.

Implement the supported operations and direct guidance together. Keep source
and trial inputs distinct from candidate-writable outputs; preserve result and
check identities. Use deliberate fixtures for malformed, absent, repeated or
conflicting events and changes of basis, then actual authorized records/trials
for the promised supported route. Examine substantive correctness, usable
representation and consumer integration as different claims. Retain failed and
uncertain results. Correct ordinary defects without weakening the governing
criteria; route a question about purpose or criteria through DEL-04-02 and the
actual owner.

At the pre-transfer Library basis, the first implementation was maintained in
`tools/undertaking_evaluation/`, with the selected practice in
`workflows/evaluate-repository-change/`. The exact recoverable source and proposed
current disposition are identified in Work/EvaluationBasis.md. Historical
production followed an actual brief and reconciled local contract, Work and
useful needs/pointers with its results.

Compare actual effects and limitations with the guidance; report unperformed claims separately. - **VER-005** — With the human/Agent 0 and DEL-04-02's selected practice, inspect one actual authorized combined use: question, useful state condition, local goal/hypothesis, supported record/execution route, identified repository states, actual outcomes/effort and supported conclusion. ## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — Useful repository state change, under relevant conditions, supplies the reason for the effort. Observation and comparison serve examination of that relationship; counts, scores, artifacts and tool operation are not substitutes for useful progress.

- **AX-003** — Treat the four perspectives as mutually corrective: representation can hide a material difference, evidence can undermine a category, the method can become burdensome, and a nominal saving can damage the intended use. No four-report ceremony or recursive observer hierarchy follows.
