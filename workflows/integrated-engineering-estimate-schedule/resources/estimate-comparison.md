# Compare an external estimate

Use this resource when the commissioned result includes comparison with another
estimate. It complements the [coupled estimating workflow](../WORKFLOW.md);
it does not replace allowance appraisal or turn an external total into a target.
Use the existing model, source records and report where they can carry the
comparison. A separate comparison register or new approval gate is not required.

## Preserve the intended independence

State whether the assignment calls for an independent construction, reproduction,
calibration from applicable evidence, or revision of a supplied estimate. These
are different purposes. For an independent construction, identify the permitted
inputs and keep comparator totals, norms and prior-run numerical selections out
of the estimating contributions until a checked independent coupled candidate
and its reasons are frozen. The assignment may intentionally share a scope
basis or selected assumption; record that common basis rather than claim broader
independence. Earlier exposure cannot be undone by a new folder or agent label.

Make the starting point of a repeat explicit. Rebuilding from original project
sources tests the definition and estimating route. Reusing a named accepted work
definition while appraising new quantities, allowances and resources tests the
estimating route. Preserve the preceding result and owner decisions within their
actual scope; do not silently inherit numerical choices or rerun initialization
over an accepted locality. A subsequent correction needs a work or evidence
reason and a retained preceding basis, not agreement with the comparator.

## Establish what is being compared

Inspect the actual source and retain an exact identity or version. Verify the
arithmetic relevant to the comparison; distinguish formulas, cached values,
literal overrides, section subtotals and the explicit total. Preserve a literal
allowance where its authorial intent is unknown. An independent recalculation
is not a native-workbook or macro-execution check. State the checks actually made.
Do not rename an unlabeled section or turn a mixed unit total into a drawing count.

Identify the subjects and qualifications needed for this use: scope and issue
maturity, included services and external contributions, quantity units and
complexity, source reuse, ordinary returns and additional work, funded duration,
price currency/date, rates, overhead/expenses and contingency. Unknown values
remain unknown. A document not separately issued may still require supporting
engineering; a third-party line may not cover the same specialist service.

Keep an unclassified source point estimate distinct from the independent
conditional-median reference, expected result and selected joint scenario.
The absence of an explicit contingency line does not establish a median.
For probabilistic results, preserve each metric's conditional meaning and the
selected scenario's actual role/quantity vector. Do not compare a task-P80 sum
as though it were a project percentile.

## Follow the work across accounting boundaries

Map comparable contributions by their actual content and responsible work home,
then present the discipline/package views the human uses. Keep the source and
independent allocations recoverable. Where work is booked differently, show an
explicit mapping or an included combined subset; do not silently move rows to
make the discipline totals agree. Mark included subsets so they cannot be added
to the project total a second time.

Trace material differences through scope, physical content, work-unit definition,
input maturity, repeated/variant work, role contributions, ordinary revisions,
additional responses, service periods and price basis. Name the populations:
a tag, distinct datasheet type, supplier package, drawing, model object, affected
touch and service week are not interchangeable units. If comparable work cannot
be isolated, retain that difference as unexplained or partly explained.

## Separate arithmetic from causal explanation

An arithmetic bridge can locate the magnitude of a fee difference without proving
its cause. For example, on a common currency and inclusion basis, the identity
`H1*r1 - H0*r0 = (H1-H0)*r0 + H1*(r1-r0)` separates an hours effect valued at
the source blend from a changed blended-price effect. State the ordering and
price/inclusion assumptions. A blended rate can combine seniority, role and
discipline mix, overhead treatment and commercial rates; call it a pure rate
change only when those other factors are held consistently.

Keep explicit expenses and external services on compatible bases. Do not add an
expense already included in the independent rate. If currency, price date or
service coverage cannot be normalized, show the amounts side by side or label a
conditional arithmetic comparison. Report a statistical-case uplift within the
independent model separately from a changed work or commercial basis.

Quantify additive scope, quantity, allocation or rate effects only to the extent
supported by a documented crosswalk and controlled recalculation. Counterfactuals
can interact and their allocation depends on order. Explain that order or retain
interaction/residual effects; do not manufacture percentages that sum to the
observed difference. Similar totals or offsetting discipline differences do not
establish equal scope, equivalent productivity or an allocation-only explanation.

## Return the comparison the owner can use

Lead with the material differences, their practical drivers and what the evidence
can resolve. Show the independent reference, expectation and recommended case
where applicable, without making them indistinguishable estimates. Preserve
conditional/unpriced work, source ambiguities and any unresolved residuals in the
same explanation. Link the detail and editable input homes rather than repeat
all source rows in the conversation.

Walk the human through the result in terms of packages and deliverables, work
quantities and scope over time. When the commissioned estimate, coupled schedule,
material sensitivities and explained comparison have been delivered and their
required checks completed, that undertaking can end. Adoption of a budget,
engineering readiness, contracting and client issue remain separate decisions.
Further optimization follows a new question or material changed evidence.
