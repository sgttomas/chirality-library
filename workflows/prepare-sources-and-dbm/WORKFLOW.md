# Prepare sources and a working DBM

Use this method for a first working Design Basis Memorandum (DBM), initial
reconciliation of an existing DBM, or a bounded update as evidence develops.
Return a checked working basis with traceable source meaning and unresolved
conditions suitable for its stated use. A working DBM is not engineering approval
or proof that its design has been completed. This method does not select the
project's decomposition, perform downstream estimating or publish a controlled
engineering issue without the corresponding assignment and authority.

## Establish the source basis and intended use

Take the purpose, selected scope, permitted writes and decision authority from
the assignment and project entry. Identify the current DBM, if any, and the
available sources: scope/RFP and attachments, process basis, calculations,
drawings, supplier or field evidence, decisions and clarifications as relevant.
These are possible contributions, not a required document portfolio. Use the
actual source roles and quality, not a fixed discipline roster or file format.

Keep source identity, revision and precise page/section/table/item locators
sufficient to examine consequential claims. Preserve original sources and
identify working derivatives. Reading views, OCR and paired native/PDF versions
are representations, not automatically independent evidence. Inspect original
figures, markups and tables where they affect meaning. When a needed extraction
or native tool is unavailable, identify the affected unreadable material and
claim; continue supported work without treating partial extraction as complete.

Distinguish stated requirements, selected decisions, technical observations,
proposals, estimating assumptions and unanswered questions. Read the substantive
disposition behind a status label. A quote is not an award, an action marked
complete is not necessarily an adopted design, and a supplied example does not
establish current scope or sizing. Preserve units, operating/design distinctions,
service, configuration, per-unit versus total quantities, case and source maturity.
Missing or conflicting evidence limits the corresponding claims; it is not
permission to fill them with invented engineering facts.

## Choose the preparation branch

**No existing DBM.** Prepare the first working basis from the supplied scope,
RFP and other available evidence. Organize it around the engineering subjects,
cases and interfaces its intended use needs. State what the sources establish,
what is proposed or assumed, and what remains unresolved. Do not fabricate a
mature process basis or require the source portfolio of a developed project.
Trace each material source requirement to its treatment or explicit gap. A
sparse source set can support a qualified planning draft while leaving design
and issue conditions open. For example, a supplied capacity requirement may
support a stated planning case while an unspecified feed composition leaves
sizing and performance claims unresolved; an example plant does not fill that gap.

**Existing DBM.** Retain its useful engineering structure and recoverable source.
For an initial reconciliation, read the whole DBM against the selected source
and decision basis. For a bounded revision, start with the changed evidence or
ruling and follow every repeated or coupled claim it affects through narrative,
tables and figures. Broaden the reading when reach is uncertain; reuse applicable
unaffected checks. A changed process case can affect both equipment duties and
utility or interface descriptions. A newer file does not automatically override
an adopted basis: resolve the actual meaning and authority of the difference.

## Reconcile claims and unresolved decisions

Connect material DBM statements to their sources and decision conditions in the
working document or existing source map. Compare in both directions: DBM claims
to their supporting sources, and applicable source requirements to DBM treatment
or explicit gaps. Separately check internal consistency among related sections,
tables, figures and cases. Internal agreement does not establish source correctness;
a missing comparison source does not by itself prove the DBM statement wrong.

Make consequences concrete: which subject is affected, what the competing
interpretations would change, and which use can or cannot proceed. Reuse existing
issue identities and answers. Apply bounded owner rulings to their actual scope;
seek only a consequential missing decision, with the competing evidence and a
proposed treatment. Continue unaffected work. Correct a shared source or decision
log only if the assignment includes that write; otherwise return the discrepancy
to its owner. The DBM does not become a duplicate decision authority.

Choose editable and delivery forms for the reader's work and required tools.
Markdown or structured text may own the working basis; a native document may
be appropriate when review, tracked changes or other required behavior warrants
it. Identify maintained sources and generated views. A linked comment, review
register or section map can help, but no fixed file bundle is required. Retain
only the information needed to review, use or recover the basis.

## Examine and return the working basis

Examine source coverage, claim support and internal consistency against the
actual saved candidate. Obtain independent scrutiny of material engineering or
numerical interpretations by a reviewer who did not author them. Give bounded
contributors their source basis, read/write limits and return route; one
integration owner examines and reconciles their findings. Apply the project's
actual authority and role constraints; TASK does not delegate.

Check the saved representation to the extent claimed: links and tables in text,
calculations where present, and required native/rendered behavior where applicable.
If a required tool or source cannot be used, state the outstanding check and
limit the result accordingly. If sources change during preparation, isolate the
affected candidate, reconcile changed meaning and repeat affected checks before
relying on it. Preserve the preceding usable basis.

Return the working DBM and its source basis, changed meaning, checks performed,
open conditions and allowed next use. A qualified first draft can be complete
for planning without settling engineering design. Unsupported portions stay
explicit; no blanket readiness follows from a successful structural check.
For interrupted work, resume from actual saved sources, draft and unresolved
findings rather than replaying edits or claiming unfinished checks passed.
Downstream work consumes this basis under its own scope and qualifications;
[define-work](../define-work/WORKFLOW.md) can clarify a resulting contribution,
while decomposition and setup apply only when independently selected.
